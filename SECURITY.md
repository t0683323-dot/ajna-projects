# Security Policy

We take security seriously and appreciate responsible disclosure.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please **do not open public issues** for suspected vulnerabilities.

Use one of the private channels below:

1. **Preferred**: GitHub Security Advisories ("Report a vulnerability" in the repository's Security tab).
2. **Email**: security@ajna-projects.org

Please include:

- Description of the vulnerability and impacted component(s)
- Reproduction steps or proof-of-concept
- Potential impact and attack scenario
- Suggested remediation (if known)
- Your contact details for follow-up

## Response SLA

We target the following response times:

- **Acknowledgement**: within **2 business days**
- **Triage decision** (severity/scope): within **5 business days**
- **Status update cadence**: at least every **7 calendar days** until resolution
- **Target remediation window**:
  - Critical: 7 calendar days
  - High: 14 calendar days
  - Medium: 30 calendar days
  - Low: 60 calendar days

Timelines may vary for complex issues, but we will communicate changes promptly.

## Disclosure Process

- We will validate and triage reported issues.
- We may ask for additional details or testing support.
- After fix deployment, we will coordinate disclosure timing with the reporter when possible.
- We will credit reporters for valid findings unless anonymity is requested.

Thank you for helping keep Ajna Projects and its users safe.
